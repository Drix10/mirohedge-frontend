Hypothesis Arena

Now

A different question. Not "can an LLM trade?" but "what must a model prove before anything it says can touch money?"

02 / The wall

Why a language model can't just trade.

i

No ground truth

A model has no feedback from the market. It can sound certain and be wrong, and never know.

ii

Old training data

What it knows ends at a cutoff. Markets don't. Anything it "remembers" may be leaked or stale.

iii

Latency

Seconds of inference are a lifetime for any edge that depends on being first.

iv

Not real time

It reads a snapshot, then reasons about a world that has already moved on.

03 / Method

Models read. Code decides.

Advisory

The model

  • Reads the world and forms hypotheses
  • Offers evidence, never instructions
  • Never touches capital directly
Authority

The system

  • Deterministic and statistical layers
  • Decide what is allowed to reach the market
  • Default to doing nothing when unsure
04 / Proof

Influence is earned, never assumed.

  1. 01
    Hypotheses are stated before they are tested.No searching for a story after the fact.
  2. 02
    Evidence has to survive realistic costs and skepticism.A good-looking backtest is not a result.
  3. 03
    A model keeps its influence only while it helps.If removing it changes nothing, it goes.

Evidence in. Capital out only if earned.
